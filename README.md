## 🧭 My Journey

> AI has never been just a subject for me — it's been a continuous journey of learning, building, and asking questions.

My journey into AI and Machine Learning began in the **first year of my B.Tech**, sparked by curiosity about how systems like ChatGPT actually work under the hood. Rather than waiting for these topics to appear in the curriculum, I got ahead of it — teaching myself Data Science and Machine Learning by looking at upcoming semester material and studying it independently.

Most of my learning came from **YouTube videos, blogs, official documentation, research papers, GitHub repositories, and open-source projects**. Whenever I picked up a new concept, my instinct was always to **build something with it** — that hands-on habit taught me far more than theory alone ever could.

As my confidence grew, I started competing in **AI and Data Science hackathons**:

- 🏫 **IIT Kharagpur Data Science Hackathon** — One of my most memorable experiences. I built a system that analyzes long stories and checks whether a character's backstory stays consistent with events in the narrative. This project pushed me deep into **retrieval systems, reasoning, and applied AI problem-solving**.
- 🤖 **Memoria** — An AI-powered personal assistant capable of managing emails, meetings, and schedules through natural language conversation. Building it taught me how modern AI applications combine **LLMs with external tools** to create genuinely useful real-world systems.
- 🧠 **GPT from scratch** — My curiosity about ChatGPT eventually led me to build my own small GPT model from the ground up. The goal was never scale — it was understanding **how language models learn from text and generate responses**. This project gave me a much deeper appreciation for the ideas behind modern AI systems.

Along the way, I've picked up recognition in several technical competitions, including becoming **Runner-Up in Neurathon (IEEE INN Community)** — a result that pushed me to keep exploring AI and ML even further.

While I've built and shipped a fair number of projects, I'm very aware there's still a **gap between implementing systems and understanding the deeper mathematical and research foundations** behind them. Closing that gap — strengthening my grasp of ML theory, model design, optimization, and current research — is the next chapter of this journey.
